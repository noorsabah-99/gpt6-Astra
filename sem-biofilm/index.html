How the reading is made

  1. The instrument data bar is cropped and pixel size is read from TIFF metadata (Thermo/FEI PixelWidth, Zeiss Image Pixel Size, Tescan PixelSizeX) or from a Hitachi/JEOL sidecar .txt dropped with the image. Otherwise measure the scale bar.
  2. Uneven brightness from charging is removed with a morphological top-hat (grey opening, then smoothing), followed by Gaussian noise smoothing.
  3. Cells are separated from the substrate with Otsu's threshold plus your offset, cleaned by binary opening, hole filling and a minimum-size filter.
  4. Touching cells are split by a marker-controlled watershed on the Euclidean distance map, seeded by h-maxima.
  5. Each object is measured by area and an ellipse fit (length, width, aspect ratio). Objects larger than 1.8× the single-cell area count as aggregates and contribute area ÷ single-cell area cells.
  6. Spatial pattern uses the Clark–Evans index R on object centroids: R < 1 clustered, ≈ 1 random, > 1 evenly spread. No edge correction is applied.

The biofilm stage is a heuristic from 2-D coverage and aggregation only. SEM shows the top surface, and dehydration shrinks cells and collapses EPS, so confirm thickness and matrix with cross-sections, CLSM or crystal-violet staining. The two images loaded at start are synthetic examples, not measurements.
